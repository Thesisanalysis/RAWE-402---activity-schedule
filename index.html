Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana

Department of Extension Education

Week 1: Analysis & Problem Identification

  • Orientation of RAWE programme to the farmers and their households.
  • Socio-economic and psychological survey of the farmers.
  • Study of existing farm situation, farmer practices, cropping pattern, subsidiary enterprises through PRA technique.
  • Agro-ecosystem analysis of the village.
  • Status of horticultural crops and agroforestry in the village.
  • Preparation of feasible and profitable farm layout/model for selected farmer.
  • Documentation of the existing situation of the farmer in the form of a report.

Week 2: Awareness & Extension Activities

  • Awareness campaigns for paddy straw management in villages.
  • Group discussion with farmers regarding PAU wheat varieties and promotion.
  • Group discussion on seasonal crop cultivation practices: weed management, nutrient deficiencies, and their management.
  • Seed treatment campaign for different crops.

Week 3: Integrated Pest Management & Kitchen Gardens

  • Group meeting on Integrated Pest Management (IPM) and conducting Farmer Field Schools.
  • Farmer interaction on purchase considerations and safe handling of pesticides.
  • Layout of a kitchen garden and integrated nutrition garden via method demonstration.
  • Establish at least 5 kitchen gardens in the village.

Week 4: Harvest & Soil/Water Analysis

  • Observing and recording farmers’ harvesting operations and yield.
  • Promotional campaign on use of PAU SMS-fitted combine harvester.
  • Method demonstration for collection of soil and water samples.
  • Collection of at least 10 soil and water samples by each student and submission to PAU soil laboratory.
  • Result demonstration of soil and water tests via individual contact method for balanced fertilizer application.
  • Awareness campaign on soil health card and its utilization.

Week 5: Horticulture & Awareness Camps

  • Lay out, planning, production management, post-harvest management, and value addition of horticultural/vegetable crops.
  • Awareness camp on nursery production practices, seed production, protected structures, and cultivation of vegetables/ornamental crops.
  • Parthenium eradication campaign.
  • Awareness camp on use of AI and drones in agriculture.

Week 6: Subsidiary Occupations & Entrepreneurship

  • Lecture-cum-interaction with farmers on different subsidiary occupations (dairy, bee-keeping, mushroom, etc.) and custom hiring of machinery.
  • Group meeting on entrepreneurship development among farm households.
  • Method demonstration on value addition of fruits, vegetables, and foodgrains.
  • Group discussion on Integrated Farming System (PAU model).
  • Farmer interaction on establishment of agro-industrial complexes by rural youth.
  • Awareness camp on Farmer Producer Organisations / Farmer Interest Groups.

Week 7: Resource Conservation & Farm Planning

  • Exhibition on Resource conservation technologies via models, specimens, or machinery.
  • Group meeting on biogas plants and solar panels.
  • Farm production plan and budgeting.

Week 8: Social Campaigns & Programme Summary

  • Campaign on social evils through students of primary and secondary schools.
  • Massive Swachh Bharat Abhiyaan campaign in the village with villagers.
  • Summarizing the whole village attachment programme via mass contact method and thanks giving function.
