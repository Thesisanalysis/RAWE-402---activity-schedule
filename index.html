Week 1: General Orientation and Village Selection (29.07.2025 to 03.08.2025)

  • Introduction of RAWE programme by Dean, COA.
  • Briefing about the programme by the Head, Dept. of Extension Education.
  • Familiarization with university farm/research stations.
  • Briefing about the village selection and its socio-economic survey.
  • Visit to village and courtesy call to village Sarpanch/Panch.

Week 2: Village Entry and Socio-Economic Survey (05.08.2025 to 10.08.2025)

  • Survey of the village and collection of socio-economic data.
  • Visit to different houses and interaction with the villagers.
  • Preparation of a village profile report.

Week 3: Crop Production Activities (12.08.2025 to 17.08.2025)

  • Visit to fields and identification of different crops.
  • Study of crop production practices in the village.
  • Discussion with farmers on improved crop varieties.
