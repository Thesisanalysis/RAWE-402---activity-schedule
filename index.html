Week 1: Analysis of the Situation and Identification of Problems

  • Orientation of RAWE programme to the farmers and their households.
  • Socio-economic and psychological survey of the farmers.
  • Study of the existing farm situation, farmer practices, cropping pattern, subsidiary enterprises of the farmers through PRA technique.
  • Agro-ecosystem analysis of the village.
  • Status of horticultural crops and agroforestry in the village.
  • Preparation of a feasible and profitable farm layout/model for the selected farmer.
  • Documentation of the existing situation of the farmer in the form of a report.

Week 2

  • Awareness campaigns/extension activities for paddy straw management in the villages.
  • Group discussion with the farmers regarding PAU wheat varieties and their promotion.
  • Group discussion with the farmers regarding seasonal crop cultivation practices viz. weed management, nutrient deficiencies and their management.
  • Seed treatment campaign for different crops.

Week 3

  • Group meeting on Integrated Pest Management (IPM) and conducting Farmer Field Schools for IPM in different crops.
  • Farmer interaction on considerations while purchase of chemicals and safe handling of pesticides.
  • Layout of a kitchen garden and integrated nutrition garden in the village through method demonstration.
  • Establish at least 5 kitchen gardens in the village.

Week 4

  • Observing and recording the farmers’ harvesting operations and yield.
  • Promotional campaign on use of PAU SMS fitted combine harvester.
  • Method Demonstration on procedure for collection of soil and water samples in the village.
  • Collection of at least 10 soil and water samples from the village by each student and submission to the PAU soil laboratory.
  • Result Demonstration of the soil and water tests through individual contact method for balanced fertilizer application.
  • Awareness campaign on soil health card and its utilization.

Week 5

  • Lay out, planning, production management, post harvest management and value addition of horticultural crops/vegetable crops in the farmers’ orchard/field.
  • Awareness camp on nursery production practices, seed production and protected structures and cultivation of vegetables/ornamental crops.
  • Parthenium eradication campaign.
  • Awareness camp on use of AI and drones in agriculture.

Week 6

  • Lecture-cum-interaction with farmers on different subsidiary occupations and custom hiring of machinery as a source of additional income/self employment among the farmers.
  • Group meeting on entrepreneurship development among farm households.
  • Method demonstration on value addition of fruits, vegetables and foodgrains.
  • Group discussion on Integrated farming system (PAU model).
  • Farmer interaction on establishment of agro-industrial complexes by rural youth.
  • Awareness camp on Farmer Producer Organisations /Farmer Interest Groups.

Week 7

  • Laying of exhibition on Resource conservation technologies for sustainable agriculture and livelihood through exhibiting models, specimens or actual machinery. Examples of successful farmers through videos.
  • Group meeting on biogas plants and solar panels.
  • Farm production plan and budgeting.

Week 8

  • Campaign on social evils through students of primary schools, secondary schools.
  • A massive Swachh Bharat Abhiyaan campaign in village along with villagers.
  • Summarizing the whole village attachment programme through mass contact method and thanks giving function for the whole village.

Evaluation

  • Attendance during the village attachment.
  • Work executed: 2-3 photographs and video recording of each activity.
  • Submission of audio-visual aids and comprehensive report presentation.
  • Development of models.
  • Practical copy.
  • Conduct and behaviour.
  • Establishment of a project in the village viz. Integrated Nutrition Garden, Mushroom cultivation, Bee-Keeping, Integrated Farming System or any other as per feasibility.
